Privacy

Sky Data, Inc.  ·  Last updated November 15, 2026

Sky Data, Inc. runs two YouTube channels, The Postmortem and Scout Learns Minecraft. We use a small internal tool to manage them through the YouTube Data API. This page explains what that tool does with Google account data.

Whose data

The tool signs in only to Google accounts that Sky Data owns. No member of the public signs in to it.

What it does

It uploads our own videos and manages their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions and privacy settings. It reads the comments people leave on our videos and posts replies as the channel.

What it does not do

It does not sell or share any data. It does not use Google user data for advertising. It keeps only what it needs to run our channels: the sign-in token for our own accounts, and the comments on our own videos while we answer them.

Google API data

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
